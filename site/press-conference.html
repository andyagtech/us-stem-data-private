The press conference, claim by claim

On 8 October 2026 the administration announced PERM suspensions for eight companies and a J-1 investigation into nine universities. Most of what was said is checkable against federal data, and this site holds much of it.

Each moment below quotes the recording, says who said it, and sets out what this site's data can and cannot show. Press a timestamp to hear it. Where our figures agree, it says so. Where they differ, it says that too.

Vice President JD Vance
This is a great day for American workers because we are taking some very concrete actions to cut down on some of the H-1B and J1 visa fraud that we've been tracking over the past several months.

What was announced

Three things followed: PERM suspensions for eight named companies, a J-1 investigation into nine named universities, and a visa-fraud strike team. The rest of this page takes the claims in the order they were made.

Vice President JD Vance
They will say, maybe they'll put an advertisement in a small town newspaper... and they will use that lack of a response to then go and replace American workers.

The recruitment test

This describes the PERM labour-market test: before sponsoring a worker for permanent residence an employer must advertise the role and show no qualified American applied. The disclosure files record that a test happened. They do not record the advertisement, the applicants, or why they were rejected, so the claim cannot be checked from published data. That gap is an ask on this site.

PERM is the step this site can see

  • 42,833certified public-employer filings, FY2013–FY2024

PERM is the only federal disclosure file that records a worker's citizenship, which is why this site can say who public employers sponsor and the H-1B files cannot. The series ends with FY2024: the Department of Labor removed every worker field from the FY2025 file, 155 columns down to 137.

Vice President JD Vance
If you are working as an H-1B visa in the United States of America, you're earning $20,000 less than an American citizen hired in the same position. If you were brought in by one of these foreign outsourcing firms, you're making $48,000 less.

The wage gap

Every wage this site holds is an OFFERED wage, because that is the only wage published. What a worker is actually paid appears on a W-2, which Treasury holds and the Department of Labor does not. So a gap of this kind cannot be confirmed or refuted from the public record, and asking for the matched tabulation is an ask on this site.

Vice President JD VancePartly differs
These universities are employing J1 visas for federally funded grants at a rate of about 61%. The national average is 38%.

The 61% checks out. The 38% does not match this survey.

  • 60.7%the nine named universities, share of postdocs holding a temporary visa
  • 58.3%every institution in the survey
  • 69,877postdoctoral appointees counted
  • 48.1%of those posts are federally supported

Computed from the NSF Survey of Graduate Students and Postdoctorates, the nine named universities come to the figure given. The national average on the same measure is not 38%. That difference decides what the story is: if the nine sat far above a low national average they would be outliers, and an enforcement action against nine institutions would address it. They do not. The American postdoctoral workforce is a minority of itself almost everywhere, and the nine look much like the rest of the country. That is the larger finding, and it is closer to the argument being made than the figure quoted.

How to read this page

The quotations are verbatim, taken from the published captions with their timestamps, so any one of them can be heard in context by pressing the time.

Six people spoke and each quotation names its speaker. Attributing one person's words to another would be the worst error this page could make.

Every figure on this page resolves from a built dataset when the page is rendered. None is typed, and a figure that stops resolving fails the build rather than appearing as a dash.

An investigation was announced. That is a fact with a date. Nothing here says any institution or company broke a law, and a citizenship share is not evidence of wrongdoing by anyone.
