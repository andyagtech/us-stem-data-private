International Student Enrollment in UK

Non-UK domicile students enrolled in UK higher education. Grew 67% from 438K (2015/16) to 732K (2023/24).

Source: HESA Student Statistics (academic years ending in year shown)

Student Visa Grants

Sponsored study visas issued. Peaked at 467K in 2022 before tightening.

India vs China Visa Grants

India surged from 18K (2018) to 138K (2022) then fell back. China stable at ~100K.

Graduate Route Visas

UK Sponsored-Study Visas Granted by Country (yearly, 2018-2025)

Top 10 countries by total over the period. China dominated early, India became #1 in 2022-23, Nigeria saw the steepest decline after the 2024 dependant ban.

UK Sponsored-Study Visas by Course Level

Masters dominates the UK student-visa pipeline — UK 1-year masters programmes are the primary front door.

UK Sponsored-Study Visas by World Region

South Asia (mainly India + Pakistan) has overtaken East Asia (mainly China) as the largest source region.
