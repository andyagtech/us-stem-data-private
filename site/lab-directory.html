Methodology

#

The shares are IPEDS field codes: a census of degrees conferred, not a count of people and not a statement about anyone's origin. A card appears where a program awarded at least 20 doctorates over the window, so the share rests on more than a handful of degrees.

Every department link was fetched and checked. A URL is listed only if it returned 200 and the page itself proved it is the right department — a university 404 handler also answers 200.

The research-group table counts people a principal investigator lists on a public laboratory page. Those pages are archived. A name-marker share is an inference from names, never a statement of anyone's citizenship, and it is given with its denominator because a share of twelve is twelve. It says how unusual a composition is and not why: a group recruits from who applies, from collaborators, from a shared language, from a technique taught in few places.

Where a department bundles several subjects, the split comes from the degree conferred, taken one record at a time from the university's own repository. Those counts are exact. Grouping the degree names into the fields named here is approximate and generated by Super Intelligence (google/gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-10-11).
