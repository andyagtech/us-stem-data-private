Tip Line

Spotted something missing or wrong? Have a useful link? Send it over.

What's most useful:

  • Links to department Students, Faculty, or Dissertations directories we don't have yet (see the Data Quality page for gaps)
  • Corrections — wrong department, wrong year, missing names
  • Datasets, federal sources, or scrapers we should add
  • Bugs, broken charts, or display issues
  • Ideas for new analyses
Up to 5,000 characters. Markdown not supported — plain text is fine.

Your message goes directly to Andy Barr's inbox. No tracking, no third-party services. Rate-limited to 3 submissions per 10 minutes per IP.

This site is built and maintained independently. If it's useful to you, consider chipping in.
