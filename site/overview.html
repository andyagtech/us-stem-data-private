Each step is a section of the site and stands on its own evidence. Read them in order, or take the short tour of any one.

  2. 2
    The master’s is a moneymaker

    Ten times the volume of the doctorate, and where the change actually happened. At 121 institutions, most of those degrees go to people who are not Americans.

    OPT authorizations grew elevenfold since 2007. The degree is the entry point to a work authorization.

What this adds up to

Public money pays for these programs, and a growing number of them now award most of their seats to people who are not Americans.

American research universities are paid for by Americans. Federal research funding runs to roughly $875,000 for every STEM research doctorate awarded, and state legislatures fund the public ones on top of that. The people who pay are entitled to ask who the money produces.

The answer is increasingly not them. At 121 of the 567 institutions large enough to measure, most of the master's degrees awarded go to people who are not Americans. At 56 institutions the same is true of the doctorate.

A seat is a finite thing. An American who is not admitted to a program does not get a smaller share of it; they get none of it. When most of a publicly funded program's seats go to temporary visa holders year after year, the effect on American applicants is the same whatever anyone intended, and the people funding it are the ones going without.
